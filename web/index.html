Each streak is one star's 1σ distance range along its line of sight. Only morph 1.000 draws the catalogue's bounds; below that the streak is a centred fraction of that range — a display transition, not a narrower measurement. Colour is a blackbody ramp keyed to observed BP−RP, uncorrected for interstellar reddening, so a reddened hot star reads cool. Intensity is a display response to G magnitude, attenuated with distance from the camera and divided along the smear; it is not photometry.
